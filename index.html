Game feel changes, in video

One card per change. BEFORE (left) = every new effect off. AFTER (right) = only that one change on - the same moment recorded twice, played in sync. No sound.

1Hit squash

A heavy hit, then a light one. After: the foe squashes along the blow, springs back past its shape and settles.

@vasilev.yegor - Disney rules"the enemy squashes on the hit"

Fish > Game Feel: Hit squash (lightSquash, heavySquash, springHz, springDamping)

2Ink impact frame

Same hits. After: on the heavy hit the foe turns into a solid ink silhouette for about 2 frames.

@vasilev.yegor - anime hit"black shapes on one"

Fish > Game Feel: Ink impact frame (inkFrameSeconds, inkFrameOnLightHits)

3Damage numbers

Same hits. After: cream digits in an ink outline fly up on an arc; heavy hits are bigger and orange.

@vasilev.yegor - spell"damage numbers - bigger for crits"

Fish > Game Feel: Damage numbers (numberSize, numberLife, numberLaunch, numberGravity)

4Recoil

Same hits. After: your fish is nudged back a little by its own blow (more on the heavy one).

@make.money.with.ai"the player bounces back"

Fish > Game Feel: Recoil (meleeRecoilSpeed, heavyRecoilScale, recoilDecay)

5Health-bar catch-up

4 light hits. After: the bar over the foe keeps a pale strip of what was just lost, holds it, then drains it down.

@porsukgames - combat"a delayed white catch-up trail"

Fish > Game Feel: Health bar catch-up (barCatchUpHold, barCatchUpSpeed, colour)

6Kill fling

A kill. Before: the body shrinks away on the spot. After: it swells for an instant, then is thrown along the blow, spinning, and lives a little longer so you see it fly.

@vasilev.yegor - shots"the last bullet has to feel the biggest"@vasilev.yegor - punch"pop it like a balloon"

Fish > Game Feel: Kill (killPop, killFlingSpeed, killSpin, killFlingDecay, killCorpseSeconds)

7Last kill of a room: slow motion

The room's last kill, real speed. After: the world runs at about a third of its speed for a moment, then eases back. The camera does not move.

@motionbykrit - barrel"drop to half speed for a split second, right at the peak"

Fish > Game Feel: Last kill of a room (finisherScale, finisherHold, finisherEase)

8Dash squash-stretch

One dash. After: the fish squashes as it sets off, stretches along the dash, and settles with a little overshoot.

@motionbykrit - dash"squash into the dash and stretch along it"

Fish > Game Feel: Dash (dashStretchAmount, dashStartSquash)

9Dash after-images

One dash. After: a trail of fading copies of the fish is left behind it.

@motionbykrit - dash"leave ghost copies behind that fade away"

Fish > Game Feel: Dash (afterimageSpacing, afterimageMax, afterimageLife, afterimageOpacity, afterimageWash)

10Prize points fly to the counter

A 6-point payout - watch the EVOLUTION card top-left. Before: the number just jumps. After: gold sparks fly to it on curves and it counts up as each lands.

@motionbykrit - coin pickup"fly the coin up to the counter, and boom! bump the number"

Fish > Game Feel: Points fly to the counter

11Healing has a price

A 40% heal. Before: you just heal. After: the game pauses on an ink card with 3 afflictions; one is picked and a tag under the EVOLUTION card shows it, one dot per room left.

@featherweight_games - Chained Beasts"when you wish to heal, you must also opt into one of a variety of afflictions"

Fish > Heal Price (enabledInRuns, offers, minHealPercent, the affliction list)

12Combo pitch (sound only)

Each hit in a quick chain plays a step higher than the last, and heavy hits get a low thud layer. It can't be shown in a silent clip - try it in the Game Feel Gym with the COMBO PITCH chip on/off.

@porsukgames - combat"climb the pitch with every hit in a row"

Fish > Game Feel: Combo pitch (comboWindow, comboPitchStep, heavyLowLayer)
